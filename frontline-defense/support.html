Need help? Email support@luxlogik.com. Tell us your device and what happened; we usually reply within two working days.

Common questions

I bought the Commander Pack but it's not unlocked.
Open Settings → Restore purchases while online, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with. If it says "Waiting for approval", the purchase needs a parent's approval (Ask to Buy) and will unlock once it is approved.
Can I move my progress to a new phone?
Progress is saved on the device. Purchases always come back with Restore purchases; stars and medals do not transfer yet.
A map is locked.
The first map of each chapter is open. Each next map opens once you earn at least one star on the map before it.
Why can't I upgrade or build something?
Tap the button anyway: the game tells you why (not enough coins, a Daily Orders rule, or the weapon unlocks on a later map). The ? button in the pause menu explains every icon.
How do I turn off usage stats or change ad consent?
Settings → Share anonymous usage stats, and Settings → Ad privacy choices (shown where required). See the privacy policy.
The game feels slow or hot on my phone.
Settings → Graphics → Battery saver turns off shadows and caps the frame rate.
