Privacy Policy

Frontline Defense · effective 31 October 2026

Frontline Defense is made by Luxlogik LTD ("we"). This policy explains what the game collects and why. Short version: we do not ask for your name, email or location, and we never sell your data.

1. Your game progress

Stars, medals, settings and purchases are saved only on your device. We have no copy of them. Deleting the app deletes them; Settings → Reset progress clears them in the game.

2. Anonymous usage statistics

To learn which maps are too hard and where players get stuck, the game sends anonymous gameplay events, for example "match started", "match ended (won, wave reached)", "store opened", "ad watched". Each event carries:

  • a random install ID created by the game (not linked to you, your Apple ID, Google account or advertising ID)
  • device type and operating system, screen size, app version
  • the game details of the event (map, difficulty, score)

These are processed by PostHog on servers in the European Union. They are used only to improve the game, never for advertising, and never combined with other data. You can turn this off at any time: Settings → Share anonymous usage stats. When it is off, nothing is sent.

3. Advertising (optional)

The game offers an optional video between waves that you may choose to watch for bonus coins. There are no other ads, and Commander Pack owners see none. These videos are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers, IP address and ad-interaction data to show and measure ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice in Settings → Ad privacy choices. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. Purchases

The Commander Pack is bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handle the payment; we never see your card or billing details. The game only learns whether the pack is owned.

5. Leaderboards and sharing

On iPhone and iPad, scores are submitted to Apple Game Center if you are signed in, and shown under your Game Center name, governed by Apple's privacy policy. The Share button creates a picture of your result on your device and opens your phone's share sheet; nothing is sent unless you choose where to send it.

6. Children

The game is suitable for a general audience but is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Retention and your rights

Anonymous usage events are kept for up to 12 months and then deleted. Because they are not linked to your identity we usually cannot single out your data, but if you have a request or question (including under GDPR or CCPA: access, deletion, objection), contact us and we will help.
